# OGRE MODELS

## Ogre Mark III
The Mark III is the "workhorse" of the Ogre line. Its track sheet structure looks like this:

- Missiles: 2 Boxes (ATK 6, RNG 5)
- Main Battery: 1 Box (ATK 4, RNG 3)
- Secondary Batteries: 4 Boxes (ATK 3, RNG 2)
- Antipersonnel: 8 Boxes (ATK 1, RNG 1)
- Tread Units: 45 Total Boxes
    Move 3: 45 to 31 treads remaining.
    Move 2: 30 to 16 treads remaining.
    Move 1: 15 to 1 treads remaining.

    
## Ogre Mark V
The Mark V is the heavy assault model, featuring a much denser sheet:

- Missiles: 6 Boxes (ATK 6, RNG 5)
- Main Batteries: 2 Boxes (ATK 4, RNG 3)
- Secondary Batteries: 6 Boxes (ATK 3, RNG 2)
- Antipersonnel: 12 Boxes (ATK 1, RNG 1)
- Tread Units: 60 Total Boxes
    Move 3: 60 to 41 treads.
    Move 2: 40 to 21 treads.
    Move 1: 20 to 1 treads.
